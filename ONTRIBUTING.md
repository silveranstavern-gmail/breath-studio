# Contributing to Breath Studio

First off, thank you for considering contributing to Breath Studio! This app is designed for practice, crafted with intention, and given with love. Whether you are fixing a bug, improving the Jetpack Compose UI, or adding a new feature, your help is deeply appreciated.

## How to Contribute
1. **Fork the repository** to your own GitHub account.
2. **Create a new branch** for your feature or bug fix (`git checkout -b feature/your-cool-feature`).
3. **Make your changes** and ensure the app still builds and runs correctly.
4. **Commit your changes** with clear, descriptive commit messages.
5. **Push to your branch** (`git push origin feature/your-cool-feature`).
6. **Open a Pull Request** against the `main` branch of this repository.
