Support Billing Setup

Breath Studio uses Google Play Billing for in-app support options.

Create these products in Play Console with matching product IDs:

Product ID: support_small
Type: One-time product
Suggested label: Support - Small

Product ID: support_medium
Type: One-time product
Suggested label: Support - Medium

Product ID: support_large
Type: One-time product
Suggested label: Support - Large

Product ID: monthly_supporter
Type: Subscription
Suggested label: Monthly Supporter

The one-time support products are consumed after purchase so a person can support more than once. The monthly supporter product is acknowledged as a subscription.

Use Play Console pricing for the real localized prices. The app includes fallback display prices only for the loading and unavailable states.

The in-app website link intentionally goes to https://ponderingsilver.com instead of directly to an external payment page.
